## Overview

`nexu-io/open-design` is an open-source collaborative AI design workspace ecosystem covering prototypes, websites, apps, dashboards, presentations, motion, and 3D scenes.

---

## 1. Curated Design System Archetypes (`references/design-systems/`)

In addition to real-world brand systems, Open Design includes purpose-built **design archetypes** for greenfield projects:
- `agentic` — Next-gen AI agent workspace & command-surface aesthetic
- `application` — Clean, high-density desktop/web application chrome
- `arc` — Atmospheric browser/spatial-canvas aesthetic with soft tinted borders
- `artistic` — Expressive gallery/creative studio direction
- `atelier-zero` — Ultra-refined luxury editorial & architectural minimalism
- `bento` — Structured modular bento-grid showcase system
- `bold` — High-impact typographic poster & kinetic contrast
- Plus 140+ brand & product systems (`airbnb`, `apple`, `claude`, `cursor`, `figma`, `framer`, `linear.app`, `notion`, `raycast`, `stripe`, `supabase`, `teenage-engineering`, `vercel`, etc.)
